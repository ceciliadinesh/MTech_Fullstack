Begin your French journey

French is spoken in many countries across Europe, Africa, North America, and other parts of the world. Begin with simple expressions that you can use every day.

USEFUL PHRASES

Everyday French

Bonjour, comment allez-vous ? Hello, how are you?
Je m'appelle Marie. My name is Marie.
Enchanté. Nice to meet you.
Vous êtes d'où ? Where are you from?
Pouvez-vous m'aider ? Can you help me?
Combien ça coûte ? How much does it cost?

GRAMMAR

French sentence basics

Subject + Verb + Object

A basic French sentence often follows the subject-verb-object structure.

Je mange du pain.
Elle lit un livre.
Nous buvons de l'eau.
