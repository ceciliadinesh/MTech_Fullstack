ENGLISH

Your English journey starts here

English is one of the most widely used languages in the world. Start with simple vocabulary and expressions that you can use while travelling, studying, working, and meeting new people.

VOCABULARY

Essential English words

Hello A greeting
Goodbye Used when leaving
Thank you To show appreciation
Please Used when asking politely
Friend A person you know well
Family People related to you
Food Things we eat
Water A common drink

USEFUL PHRASES

Everyday English

Hello, how are you? A friendly greeting
My name is Alex. Use this to introduce yourself.
Nice to meet you. Use this when meeting someone.
Where are you from? Ask someone about their country or city.
Could you help me? A polite way to ask for help.
How much does it cost? Useful when shopping.

GRAMMAR

English sentence basics

Subject + Verb + Object

A basic English sentence commonly follows this pattern.

I eat food.
She reads books.
They play football.
