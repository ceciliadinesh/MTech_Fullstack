SPANISH

Start speaking Spanish

Spanish is spoken across Spain, Latin America, and many communities around the world. Start with common words and phrases that you can use in everyday situations.

USEFUL PHRASES

Everyday Spanish

Hola, ¿cómo estás? Hello, how are you?
Me llamo Ana. My name is Ana.
Mucho gusto. Nice to meet you.
¿De dónde eres? Where are you from?
¿Puedes ayudarme? Can you help me?
¿Cuánto cuesta? How much does it cost?

GRAMMAR

Spanish sentence basics

Subject + Verb + Object

Spanish commonly uses a subject followed by a verb and an object.

Yo como pan.
Ella lee libros.
Nosotros bebemos agua.
